Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 3rd, 2026

At Appnest we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our app. By using Our Apps you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you use our app:

  • Device Information: We may collect basic information about your device, such as device type, operating system, and unique device identifiers, to optimize app performance and deliver personalized content.
  • Usage Data: We collect information about how you interact with the app, such as the features you use and the music tracks you select, to improve your experience.
  • Push Notification Data: Through our use of OneSignal, we may collect data necessary to send you push notifications, such as device tokens or preferences, if you opt in to receive them.
  • Ad-Related Data: Through Google AdMob, we may collect information like your IP address, advertising ID, and browsing behavior to serve personalized ads and monetize the app.

We do not collect sensitive personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number unless you voluntarily provide it (e.g., when contacting us).

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide and improve the Ringtones MP3 app and its features.
  • To send you push notifications about app updates, new ringtones, or promotions (via OneSignal) if you enable this feature.
  • To display personalized advertisements through Google AdMob and generate revenue to keep the app free for users.
  • To analyze app usage and performance to enhance your experience.

Third-Party Services

We partner with the following third-party services, which may collect and process data as described below:

  • OneSignal: We use OneSignal to send push notifications. If you opt in, OneSignal may collect device information and notification preferences. For more details, please review OneSignal’s Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob: We use Google AdMob to display ads in the app. AdMob may collect data such as your advertising ID and IP address to provide personalized ads. You can learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy.

These third parties have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

Your Choices

  • Push Notifications: You can enable or disable push notifications in your device settings or within the app.
  • Ad Personalization: You can opt out of personalized ads by adjusting your device’s advertising settings (e.g., resetting your advertising ID or opting out of ad tracking).

Data Sharing

We, at KD Creation, do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • With OneSignal and Google AdMob, as described above, to provide app functionality and ads.
  • When required by law or to protect the safety and rights of our users and the public.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no app or online service can guarantee absolute security, and we encourage you to keep your device secure.

Children’s Privacy

Ringtones MP3 is not intended for children under 13. KD Creation does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have unintentionally collected such data, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated “Last Updated” date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
